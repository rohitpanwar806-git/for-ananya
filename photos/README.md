### How to save them
1. Open the image full-size, then **right-click → Save image as…** (on a phone: long-press → Download).
2. Save it into this `photos/` folder.
3. Rename it to `photo1.jpg`, `photo2.jpg`, or `photo3.jpg` as in the table above.
4. Refresh the website — your photos appear automatically. ✨

Tips:
- `.jpg`, `.jpeg` or `.png` all work — just keep the names above.
- `photo2` and `photo3` are shown as tall portraits (perfect for those selfies).
- Until you add them, the site shows a pretty "Add your photo here" placeholder — nothing breaks.
